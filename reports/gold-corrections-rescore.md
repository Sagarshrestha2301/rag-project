## Method

1. Rebuilt the corrected set from the three field edits (55 records, same order).
2. Scored each of the **17** `eval/runs/*/run.jsonl` twice with `eval/score.py` — once
   against `eval/questions.jsonl`, once against the corrected copy — writing to a scratch
   output folder that was deleted afterwards.
3. Compared per question, not just totals, so a flip would be visible even if the count
   happened to cancel.

## What the comparison proves

- **One question changes, everywhere it can:** `q042` is the only fail→pass in all 17 runs,
  and there is **no pass→fail in any run**. q044's corrected quote and q050's corrected
  answer move nothing, because every run already cites `Kakani- Kathmandu` and already
  states the 11-15 m range.
- **Citation is unchanged in every run.** The correction does not loosen
  `citation_correct`; it makes one recorded quote match the page's own characters.
- **The two runs that stay level** are the held-out-only run (10 rows, 11/55 by
  construction) and the smoke run; q042 is not answered correctly in either, so there is
  nothing to flip.
- **The two `2026-11-17-step6-heldout`/smoke cases** are untouched, so the +1 is not a
  blanket constant applied to a total: it is the same single question in 15 different runs.

## Why the relative conclusions still hold

The delta is uniform, so comparisons made on the old gold stay valid on the new one:

- accepted baseline vs wave-01: **37 vs 33 = 4 questions apart** before, **38 vs 34 = 4**
  after — the wave-01 rollback decision does not change.
- the three 37/55-era runs (baseline, rerank-anchor1, rerank-top10) all restate to 38/55
  together, so their ranking is unchanged.
- dev/held-out splits and not-found (a) are untouched by these edits.

## Caveat

These are projections against a **draft** gold set. Until the owner applies
`docs/gold-corrections-2026-11-24.md`, the filed `scores.json` of every run remains the
37/55-family figure, and this table must not be quoted as the current baseline. After

regenerated by hand before `eval/test_scorer.py` is run again.
